# Data notice

The archive omits the frozen model registry and the two Agent Arena snapshots used by the manuscript. The local files are `arena_snapshot_2026-10-08.json` and `arena_full_2026-10-02.json`; their source metadata records retrieval by parsing the rendered leaderboard payload. Arena's current Terms of Use restrict programmatic access, automated extraction, reproduction, and redistribution of service output. The authors have not received permission to redistribute these local snapshots, so they are not included here.

The registry also contains third-party observations and derived fields from OpenRouter, Hugging Face, and Artificial Analysis. It is omitted pending source-by-source rights review. This means the archive does not, by itself, reproduce the paper's empirical tables.

Arena publishes a historical leaderboard dataset on Hugging Face with a CC BY 4.0 label: <https://huggingface.co/datasets/lmarena-ai/leaderboard-dataset>. The authors have not verified that its released rows reproduce the paper's exact 2026-10-08 headline values and registry joins. Treat it as an alternative source for a documented rerun, not as the study's frozen input.
